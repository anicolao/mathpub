\begin{block}{Summary \& Key Takeaways}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{FICO Score Range}: Scores range from 300 to 850; higher scores unlock lower interest rates and better loan terms.
    \item \textbf{Top Categories}: Payment history (35\%) and amounts owed (30\%) have the largest general-population weights.
    \item \textbf{Utilization}: Lower reported utilization is generally better; \textbf{30\% is not a scoring cutoff}.
    \item \textbf{Credit Monitoring}: Review your 3 credit reports regularly to verify accuracy and detect errors early.
  \end{itemize}
\end{block}
